# For Sakshi ♡ — Final Version

The finished birthday website for Sakshi, with every latest touch. This is the **unlocked** version — it opens straight onto the intro screen, no countdown lock.

## What's inside

- `index.html` — the whole site (open it in any browser, or host the folder as-is)
- `assets/` — all photos, the photo sphere images and the birthday song

## Highlights of this version

- The full site: intro, hero, love letter, blow-the-candle wish, timeline, Our moments cascade, the 154-photo memories sphere with a soft pink aura and a caption for every photo, quiz, Open-when letters, coupons, the floating-bubble gift and the finale
- A flock of tiny ASCII butterflies in the page's colours wandering around the cake — tap one and it darts away; tap the cake and they all scatter, then new ones drift in
- The finale's "Happy Birthday, Sakshi!" heading covered in a blanket of tiny butterflies — one tap sends them flying off the screen and reveals the text
- A "Touch this" hint drifting beside the candle, the gift inside a floating bubble that pops when opened, and falling blossoms throughout
